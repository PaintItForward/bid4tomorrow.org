Our Story

Art That Gives Back

Founded by student artists and youth organizers in the Greater Toronto Area, Bid For Tomorrow began with a simple belief: creativity can do more than inspire — it can fund real-world change for youth, healthcare, and humanitarian causes.

The Movement

Connecting Artists & Communities

Bid For Tomorrow is a student-led international organization connecting young artists, student leaders, and communities through fine art and transparent philanthropy.

Through museum-grade student-led art auctions, curated exhibitions, and community studio initiatives, we give young artists a prestigious platform to share their creations while directing 100% of auction proceeds toward audited grassroots causes.

As we expand, we are building a united network of student-led chapters across cities worldwide — spanning Toronto, Lexington, and beyond.

Our Core Foundation

The Principles That Guide Us

Every auction, workshop, and chapter launch operates under four foundational commitments.

Direct Impact

Each auction sale is split between the student artist and charitable causes, helping both young creators and communities in need.

Curatorial Prestige

We treat youth and student art with museum-standard catalogue presentation, archival conservation, and professional live auctions.

Youth-Led Autonomy

Branches are completely founded and operated by local high school and university students, fostering lifelong leadership.

Cross-Border Network

Inter-chapter exchanges allow artists in Toronto, Massachusetts, and new city branches to exhibit in each other's student-led art auctions that connect creativity with charitable giving.
