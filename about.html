Our Story

Art That Gives Back

Bid For Tomorrow began with a simple idea: what if art could do more?

Gloria was drawn to the excitement of auctions and the way a piece of art could bring people together. She began wondering how that same idea could be used to support causes that matter.

That became Bid For Tomorrow — giving student artists a platform to showcase and sell their work while knowing that their creativity is also helping others.

Brian and Gloria connected through their shared passion for art and a belief that creating a piece should be about more than the piece itself. Art can create opportunities, bring people together, and make an impact.

We're starting with student artists and causes supporting youth physical and mental health, with a bigger goal ahead: to build a worldwide initiative where creativity gives back.

The Movement

Connecting Artists & Communities

Bid For Tomorrow is a student-led nonprofit connecting young artists, student leaders, and communities through art auctions and charitable giving.

Through student-led art auctions and creative initiatives, we give young artists a platform to share their creations while using their creativity to support youth physical and mental health.

As we expand, we are building a growing network of student-led chapters, beginning with Toronto and Lexington.

Our Core Foundation

The Principles That Guide Us

Every auction, workshop, and chapter launch operates under four foundational commitments.

Charitable Impact

Auction proceeds are directed toward charitable causes supported by Bid For Tomorrow. We maintain transparent financial tracking and reporting.

Student Art & Opportunity

We give student artists meaningful opportunities to share their work, build experience, and connect their creativity with charitable impact.

Youth-Led Leadership

Our student-led structure gives young people opportunities to take initiative, build leadership skills, and contribute to their communities.

Cross-Border Network

Inter-chapter exchanges allow artists in Toronto, Massachusetts, and new city branches to exhibit in each other's charity galas.
