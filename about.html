About Bid For Tomorrow

We believe student art can do more.

Bid For Tomorrow is a student-led nonprofit that gives young artists a platform to share their work while using art to support causes that matter.

Our Story

Where it started

What if a student could sell their artwork and know that the money was helping someone else?

We wanted to create more than an art show. We wanted to give student artists a real platform — one where their work could be appreciated, purchased, and used to create meaningful impact.

That idea became Bid For Tomorrow.

What We Do

Three ways we make an impact

For Artists

A platform for young artists to exhibit and sell their work.

For Communities

A way to bring people together around art and meaningful causes.

For Causes

Fundraising driven by young people and their creativity.

How It Works

A simple process

01

Create

Students create and submit their artwork.

02

Exhibit

Artwork is presented through Bid For Tomorrow.

03

Auction

People bid on and purchase student artwork.

04

Give

Funds support causes focused on creating a better tomorrow for young people and communities.

Our Mission

Give young artists a platform to share their creativity while using that creativity to help others.
