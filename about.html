About Us

Art That Gives Back

Paint It Forward is a student-led international organization connecting young artists, student leaders, and communities through art and philanthropy.

Founded by students in the Greater Toronto Area, Paint It Forward began with a simple belief: creativity can do more than inspire — it can create meaningful change. Through student-led art auctions, exhibitions, and community initiatives, we give young artists a platform to share their work while supporting causes that matter.

As we grow, we are building a network of student-led chapters across cities. From Toronto to Manhattan and beyond, our goal is to bring young people together around a shared passion for creativity, community, and giving back.
