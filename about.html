About Us

Founded by students in the Greater Toronto Area, Paint It Forward began with a simple idea: use student creativity to create meaningful change.

About Us

Art That Gives Back

Paint It Forward is a student-led international organization connecting young artists, student leaders, and communities through art and philanthropy.

Through student-led art auctions, exhibitions, and community initiatives, we give young artists a platform to share their work while supporting causes that matter.
