Artist Agreement

Version: v1.0-draft  ·  Effective: TBD (pending legal review)
This is an early draft prepared to support platform development. It has not been reviewed or approved by a lawyer and is subject to change. Do not treat this as a final agreement. Some items are intentionally left as [TO BE CONFIRMED] and must be resolved by counsel before use.

1. Parties

This agreement is between Bid For Tomorrow (“we”, “us”, “the organization”) and the submitting artist (the “artist”). Where the artist is under 18 years of age, this agreement is entered into by the artist’s parent or legal guardian (the “guardian”), who accepts these terms on the artist’s behalf. In that case, references to “the artist” include the guardian where the context requires.

2. What this agreement covers

The artist is submitting one or more original artworks to be considered for display and auction through the Bid For Tomorrow Fall 2026 auction. This agreement applies to each artwork submitted by the artist and accepted into the auction.

3. Originality and authority

The artist confirms that:

  • Each submitted artwork is the artist’s own original creation.
  • The artist has the right to submit the artwork and to grant the permissions in this agreement.
  • The artwork does not knowingly infringe anyone else’s rights.
  • The artwork has not been sold or committed exclusively to another auction or buyer.

4. Display and promotional use

The artist grants Bid For Tomorrow permission to:

  • Display the artwork and its submitted image, title, medium, dimensions, and description on the auction site.
  • Use those materials to promote the auction, including on social media and in printed or digital materials related to the organization’s charitable activities.
  • Retain a record of the submitted materials for administrative and reporting purposes.

This permission is limited to purposes connected to the auction and to the organization’s charitable activities. It does not transfer ownership of the artwork or its copyright.

5. Proceeds arrangement

The proceeds from a sold artwork are split as follows:

  • 50% to the artist (paid to the guardian where the artist is a minor, or to the account designated by the artist or guardian).
  • 50% to Bid For Tomorrow in support of its charitable activities.

This split is currently the same for all artworks in the Fall 2026 auction. If it changes for a future auction or branch, we will state the change clearly before bidding opens.

6. Payment to the artist

After the auction closes, after the winning bidder’s payment clears, and after any required administrative steps are complete, the artist’s share will be paid to the designated account. The exact timing and method are [TO BE CONFIRMED] and will be described in a follow-up communication or in an updated version of this agreement. For minor artists, payment is made to the parent or legal guardian.

7. Delivery and fulfillment

Artworks are handed over in person. Shipping is not offered. The winning bidder is responsible for collecting the artwork at the time and place we communicate after the auction closes. If a winning bidder cannot reasonably collect the artwork, we may, at our discretion, arrange an alternative handoff. Any cost the organization chooses to cover in such a case is discretionary and not a guaranteed service. The artist or guardian is responsible for making the artwork available for handoff.

8. If the artwork does not sell

If an artwork does not receive a qualifying bid, it will be returned to the artist through the same offline channel used for submission. It will not be re-listed in a future auction without a new agreement or an updated acceptance from the artist or guardian.

9. Withdrawal and cancellation

The artist or guardian may withdraw an artwork before bidding opens on that artwork. Once bidding has opened, withdrawal is not normally permitted. If an artwork is withdrawn after bidding for reasons outside the organization’s control, any winning bids will be cancelled and any payment returned. The organization may also cancel a listing if it cannot be administered properly.

10. Publication and approval

An artwork is not published on the auction site until:

  • This agreement has been accepted (and, for a minor artist, a parent or guardian consent has been recorded); and
  • An authorized administrator has approved the artwork for publication.

Submission of an artwork through our intake channel does not by itself create a public listing.

11. Privacy

Our handling of personal information is described in the Privacy Notice. Guardian contact information collected for a minor artist is used only to verify consent and to administer payment and handoff. It is not published.

12. Relationship to other terms

This agreement is separate from the Bidder Terms & Conditions, which apply to bidders. If the artist or guardian is also a bidder, those terms apply to bidding activity.

13. Governing law and jurisdiction

This agreement will be governed by the laws of [TO BE CONFIRMED], and, where applicable, the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, United States. The specific jurisdiction and any cross-border considerations must be confirmed by counsel, including because the organization operates in Canada and plans a branch in Lexington, Massachusetts.

14. Changes to this agreement

We may update this agreement. If we make a material change, we will publish a new version identifier and ask the artist or guardian to accept the updated version before the next submission or, where relevant, before the next bid on the affected artwork. Previous acceptances remain on record.
