Bid For Tomorrow

Creativity doesn't stop at one city.

Bid For Tomorrow is growing through student-led branches that bring our mission into their own communities. Each branch works locally while contributing to the larger Bid For Tomorrow movement.

01 Founding Branch

Toronto

Greater Toronto Area · Canada

Toronto is where Bid For Tomorrow began. As our founding branch, the Toronto team is responsible for pioneering our first auction model, building relationships with local artists and organizations, and establishing the foundation for future branches.

Country Canada
Focus Founding & Operations
Current Initiative Toronto Art Auction
02 U.S. Branch

Lexington

Lexington, Massachusetts · United States

The Lexington branch brings Bid For Tomorrow's model to a new community in the United States. The branch will work with local students and artists to develop its own community presence while staying connected to the broader Bid For Tomorrow organization.

One organization, local impact

Every branch has its own community.

Branches are locally led, but connected by the same mission, standards, and vision.

1

Build Locally

Branches develop relationships with students, artists, organizations, schools, and community members in their area.

2

Create Impact

Each branch can organize initiatives and fundraising activities that reflect the needs and opportunities of its community.

3

Grow Together

Branches share ideas, resources, and experience while contributing to the larger Bid For Tomorrow network.

The next chapter

More communities. More artists. More impact.

Toronto and Lexington are only the beginning. As Bid For Tomorrow grows, we hope to build a network of student-led branches that use creativity to make a meaningful difference in their communities.
