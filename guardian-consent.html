1. Purpose

This consent allows a minor artist (under 18) to participate in the Bid For Tomorrow Fall 2026 auction. It is completed by the artist’s parent or legal guardian (the “guardian”). It records the guardian’s acknowledgement of the Artist Agreement, the Bidder Terms & Conditions, and the Privacy Notice, and the guardian’s consent for the artist to participate.

4. What the guardian is consenting to

By completing this consent, the guardian confirms that they:

  • Have read the current version of the Artist Agreement and accept it on the artist’s behalf.
  • Have read the Bidder Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Notice.
  • Consent to the artist’s participation in the auction.
  • Consent to the artwork(s) being displayed on the auction site and used in related promotion, as described in the Artist Agreement.
  • Consent to the proceeds arrangement described in the Artist Agreement, including the 50/50 split and payment to the guardian on the artist’s behalf.
  • Understand that an artwork is not published until this consent is recorded and an administrator approves the artwork.

5. What the guardian is not consenting to

This consent does not:

  • Transfer ownership or copyright of the artwork.
  • Permit use of the artwork or the artist’s personal information for purposes unrelated to the auction and the organization’s charitable activities.
  • Waive any right the guardian or artist may have under applicable law.
  • Substitute for any additional consent or authorization that may be required by the laws of a particular jurisdiction.

7. Version being accepted

This consent refers to Artist Agreement v1.0-draft. If the Artist Agreement is updated with a material change, we will ask the guardian to accept the updated version before the affected artwork proceeds.

8. Timestamp and record

The date and time the guardian completed this consent is recorded automatically when the consent is submitted through our system. A record of this consent is kept alongside the artist’s submission. Only authorized administrators can view it.

9. Withdrawal of consent

The guardian may withdraw this consent before bidding opens on the affected artwork. After bidding opens, withdrawal is not normally permitted, but the guardian should contact us if there are exceptional circumstances. If consent is withdrawn before bidding, the artwork will not be published.

10. Cross-border considerations

The organization operates in Canada and plans a branch in Lexington, Massachusetts, United States. Consent requirements for minors can vary between jurisdictions. This consent is intended to satisfy our standard practice but may not satisfy every jurisdiction. Counsel should review whether additional steps are required for artists or guardians located in either country.
