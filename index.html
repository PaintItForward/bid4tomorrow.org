Our Mission

When youth compassion meets creative expression, small acts of kindness become lasting community care.

Bid For Tomorrow was founded on the conviction that high school artists have profound potential to effect direct change. Through public exhibition auctions, we connect emerging youth creators across the Greater Toronto Area and New England with our designated charity partner.

Our Sustainable Model

Every auction dollar is split 50/50. Half funds Holland Bloorview Kids Rehabilitation Hospital — our sole beneficiary — and half goes directly to the student artist for continuing education and creative supplies.

01

Creativity as Agency

Imagination is one of the most potent tools young people possess. We elevate classroom work into public exhibitions that command respect and financial impact.

02

Direct Health Advocacy

Every canvas painted and every bid placed funds tangible pediatric rehabilitation resources at Holland Bloorview Kids Rehabilitation Hospital.

03

Student Leadership Right Now

Students curate our galleries, manage event operations, and direct community outreach — proving youth lead through immediate action.

How It Works

The whole process, start to finish — no fine print, no confusing steps.

01

Students Create

High school artists from our GTA and Lexington chapters submit original work — paintings, drawings, mixed media, anything made by hand.

02

We Exhibit

Selected pieces go up on our online gallery and at in-person events, with artist, medium, dimensions, and starting bid for every lot.

03

You Bid

Anyone can register as a bidder — no membership required. Browse lots online or at an event and place your bid.

04

Creativity Creates Impact

Half funds Holland Bloorview. Half goes directly to the student artist for supplies and education. Every sale is a 50/50 split.
