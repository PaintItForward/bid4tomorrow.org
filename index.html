Our Story

Art as a Force for Good

Bid For Tomorrow was created from the belief that art should connect people, tell stories, and create change. As students, we have seen how creativity can give people a voice, bring communities together, and turn even small acts of kindness into something meaningful.

Through student-led art auctions, we sell student-created artwork to raise funds and awareness for causes that support people facing real challenges in the community. Our work focuses on supporting causes connected to youth, creativity, health, and community care.

We believe that when young people come together with compassion and creativity, art becomes a powerful way to give back.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every artwork, every auction, every act of giving.

Creativity
We believe imagination is one of the most powerful tools young people have to shape the world around them.
Compassion
Every canvas we paint, every bid placed, is an act of care for people in our community who need support.
Community
Art is not made in isolation — it is a bridge that connects artists, supporters, and causes in meaningful ways.
Leadership
Young people are not the leaders of tomorrow — they are leading right now, through every brushstroke and bold idea.
Giving Back
Every artwork we create, every dollar we raise, goes back to the people and causes that need it most.

Who We Support

Our Supported Causes

As Bid For Tomorrow grows, we hope to direct our fundraising toward these impactful organizations. Each cause reflects our commitment to youth, creativity, health, and community care across North America and beyond.

Toronto Arts Foundation
Helping artists thrive and making arts accessible to all communities across Toronto through programs and partnerships.
SickKids Foundation
Supporting Canada's most research-intensive hospital, giving hope to children and families facing the toughest health challenges.
Holland Bloorview Foundation
Canada's largest pediatric rehabilitation hospital, creating a world of belonging for children and youth with disabilities.
Kids Help Phone
Canada's only 24/7 e-mental health service offering counselling and information to young people in both official languages.
