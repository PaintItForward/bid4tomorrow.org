This is an early draft prepared to support platform development. It has not been reviewed or approved by a lawyer and is subject to change. Do not treat this as a final agreement.

1. Who these terms apply to

These terms apply to any person who creates an account on Bid For Tomorrow and participates in an auction as a bidder. By registering an account and accepting these terms, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old, or that you have the consent of a parent or legal guardian to participate.

2. The role of Bid For Tomorrow

Bid For Tomorrow operates a platform that displays artwork submitted by student artists and allows registered bidders to place bids. Bid For Tomorrow is not the seller of the artwork. The participating artist or their parent/guardian is the seller. Bid For Tomorrow facilitates the auction and coordinates handoff.

3. Bids are binding

When you place a bid, you are making a binding offer to purchase the artwork at the amount you entered if you are the winning bidder. You may not retract a bid once it is placed. If you are the winning bidder, you agree to complete payment within the period we specify at auction close.

4. Bid eligibility and increments

Bids must meet the minimum amount shown on the artwork page and must respect the published bid increment. Bids below the minimum are rejected. The platform may cancel or reject bids that appear fraudulent, automated, or contrary to these terms.

5. Auction timing

Each auction has a published start and end time. Bids placed after the end time will not be accepted. We may extend, postpone, or cancel an auction if technical or administrative issues require it.

6. Payment

The exact payment method, timing, and handling will be described at auction close. By bidding, you agree to complete payment promptly if you win. If payment is not completed, the artwork may be offered to the next highest bidder or returned to the artist, and your account may be restricted.

7. Artwork condition and authenticity

All artworks are original student creations. The artist or their parent/guardian represents that they have the right to submit the artwork and that it is their own work. Artworks are sold as shown. No refunds or exchanges after purchase unless required by applicable law.

8. Proceeds

The proceeds arrangement between the artist and Bid For Tomorrow will be described on the artwork or auction page and in the artist agreement. The organization operates on a nonprofit basis; a portion of proceeds supports audited youth healthcare initiatives. The exact split may vary by auction or branch and will be disclosed before bidding opens.

9. Delivery, pickup, and fulfillment

After payment, the artwork will be made available for pickup, delivery, or shipping according to the arrangement described at auction close. Any shipping costs, customs, or related charges will be disclosed before you bid or before payment.

10. Withdrawal and cancellation

An artist or guardian may withdraw an artwork before bidding opens. Once bidding opens, withdrawal is not normally permitted. If an artwork is withdrawn after bidding for reasons outside our control, winning bids will be cancelled and any payment returned.

11. Privacy

Our handling of your personal information is described in the Privacy Notice. That notice forms part of these terms.

12. Acceptable use
